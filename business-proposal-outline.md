# Business Proposal Outline

**Category:** Correspondence  
**Tags:** `[Proposals]` `[Shariah]`

---

## Description

Generate a structured business proposal outline for pitching Fiqh Tech's Shariah-compliant digital solutions to potential clients.

## When to Use

- Preparing a pitch for an Islamic bank, mosque administration, or halal SME
- Creating a proposal template for the sales team
- Drafting a grant or partnership application

---

## Prompt Template

```
Create a business proposal outline for Fiqh Tech pitching [solution/service name]
to [target client type, e.g. Islamic bank / halal SME / mosque administration].

Structure it with:
1. Executive summary (Shariah compliance emphasis)
2. Problem statement (framed around Islamic business principles)
3. Proposed solution + key features
4. Shariah compliance assurance section
5. Pricing / engagement model
6. Team credentials
7. Next steps & CTA

Use a professional but accessible tone.
Highlight trustworthiness and community impact.
```

---

## Example Input

```
Solution: Digital Zakat Management System
Target client: State Islamic Religious Department (JAIS / MAIS)
```

## Expected Output Format

A numbered outline with:
- Section headers
- 3–5 bullet points per section
- Notes on tone or emphasis where relevant
