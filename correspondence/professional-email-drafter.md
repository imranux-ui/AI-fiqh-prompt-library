# Professional Email Drafter

**Category:** Correspondence  
**Tags:** `[Email]` `[Shariah]`

---

## Description

Compose professional, culturally appropriate emails on behalf of Fiqh Tech — with optional Islamic greeting and Malaysian business context.

## When to Use

- Following up after a client meeting or demo
- Sending a partnership inquiry
- Welcoming a new client onboard
- Any formal business correspondence

---

## Prompt Template

```
Draft a professional email on behalf of Fiqh Tech for the following purpose:

Purpose: [e.g. follow-up after a proposal / onboarding welcome / partnership inquiry]
Recipient: [client name / title / organisation]
Key points to include: [bullet your main points]
Preferred salutation style: [formal / Islamic greeting / neutral]

Keep the tone respectful, concise, and culturally appropriate for a Malaysian Islamic tech context.
Length: under 200 words unless stated otherwise.
```

---

## Example Input

```
Purpose: Follow-up after initial product demo
Recipient: Encik Ahmad, Head of Finance, Amanah Credit Union
Key points: Thank him for his time, confirm interest in the halal portfolio tool, propose a second meeting
Salutation: Islamic greeting
```

## Expected Output Format

- Subject line
- Greeting (with or without Islamic salutation)
- 2–3 paragraph body
- Professional sign-off with Fiqh Tech branding
