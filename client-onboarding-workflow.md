# Workflow: Client Onboarding

**Type:** Multi-step chain  
**Steps:** 3  
**Estimated time:** 20–30 minutes

---

## Overview

A 3-step chain that takes a new Fiqh Tech client from first contact to a full welcome package — proposal, onboarding email, and product FAQ.

---

## When to Use

- Onboarding a newly signed client
- Preparing materials for a prospective client after a successful demo
- Standardising the onboarding experience across the team

---

## Step-by-Step

### Step 1 — Draft the proposal
**Prompt to use:** `prompts/correspondence/business-proposal-outline.md`  
**Input:** Client name, sector (e.g. Islamic microfinance, halal SME), solution being pitched  
**Output:** A structured proposal outline tailored to the client's context

---

### Step 2 — Send the welcome email
**Prompt to use:** `prompts/correspondence/professional-email-drafter.md`  
**Input:** Client name and details from Step 1  
**Purpose:** "Onboarding welcome — confirming next steps after signed agreement"  
**Salutation:** Islamic greeting if appropriate  
**Output:** Warm, professional welcome email with clear next steps

---

### Step 3 — Build their FAQ page
**Prompt to use:** `prompts/support/faq-knowledge-base-builder.md`  
**Input:** The specific Fiqh Tech product(s) the client has access to  
**Target user:** The client's end users (e.g. mosque treasurer, SME finance manager)  
**Output:** 5–10 FAQ entries ready to paste into a help centre or onboarding pack

---

## Notes

- Personalise each step with the client's name and sector — generic outputs undermine trust
- For clients in sensitive sectors (Islamic banking, waqf management), flag FAQ entries that may require a scholar's review
