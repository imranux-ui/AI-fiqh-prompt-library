# Content Calendar Generator

**Category:** Content  
**Tags:** `[Content]` `[Scheduling]`

---

## Description

Build a structured monthly social media content plan aligned to Islamic calendar events and Fiqh Tech's focus areas.

## When to Use

- Planning monthly social content in advance
- Aligning posts with Ramadan, Eid, or other Islamic dates
- Coordinating content across multiple platforms

---

## Prompt Template

```
Create a 4-week social media content calendar for Fiqh Tech for the month of [month / hijri month].

For each week, suggest:
- 3 post ideas (topic + platform + format: carousel / reel / static)
- 1 community engagement post (poll, question, or story)
- Key dates to acknowledge (Islamic holidays, national events in Malaysia)

Company focus areas: Shariah-compliant fintech, digital Islamic education, halal business tools.
Tone: modern, professional, values-driven.
```

---

## Example Input

```
Month: Ramadan (Bulan 9 Hijri)
```

## Expected Output Format

A week-by-week table or list with:
- Week number and date range
- 3 post ideas per week (topic, platform, format)
- 1 engagement post idea
- Notable Islamic dates flagged
